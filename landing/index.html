Three steps, about ten seconds, no forms and no sign-up.

Our six checks

Official licence listsSEC, pension regulator and newly licensed firms.

About Veridex

Investment scams reach people on WhatsApp, Telegram and the phone, at the moment they are most hopeful. Checking should be just as easy: no website to visit, no jargon to decode.

Veridex was built for the SEC–ALX Virtual & Digital Assets Hackathon 2026. It gives people a simple answer in their own language, and it gives the SEC a clear view of the scams people are being offered.

Frequently asked questions

Straight answers.

Can you tell me if an investment is safe?

No one can promise that. We tell you whether the company is on official lists and whether the offer shows known scam warning signs. A green result means "looks licensed", not "guaranteed". Always confirm with the SEC on 0800 100 065 before you pay.

Is Veridex an official SEC service?

No. Veridex is an independent project built for the SEC–ALX hackathon. We use public information published by the SEC and other regulators. For official confirmation, contact the SEC directly.

Is it free?

Yes. Checking an offer costs you nothing.

What happens to my message?

We save the text you send and your chat ID, so we can spot scams that keep returning. If you press "Report this to the SEC", your report is passed to the SEC team. Never send passwords, PINs, OTP codes or your Ghana Card number.

I already paid. What do I do?

Act fast, because the first hours matter most. Call your mobile money provider or bank right away and tell them it was a scam. Then report it to the Cyber Security Authority on 292 and to the SEC on 0800 100 065. Keep every screenshot and receipt.

Which languages do you support?

English, Twi, Ga, Ewe, Fante and Dagbani, as text and as a spoken answer. Local-language voice is still being tested, so some answers may come in English first.

I do not have a smartphone or internet. Can I still use it?

That is what we are building next: a phone-call line and a USSD code, so anyone with any phone can check an offer. They are on our roadmap and not live yet.
